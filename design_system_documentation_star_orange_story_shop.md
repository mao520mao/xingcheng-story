# Celestial Citrus Dream (星橙故事铺) Design System

## 1. Brand Essence
- **Name**: 星橙故事铺 (Star Orange Story Shop)
- **Mascot**: 星橙 (Star Orange) - A glowing star wearing an orange peel hat with a small green leaf.
- **Core Value**: Peaceful, healing, whimsical, and safe bedtime stories for children (8-13 years old).
- **Visual Style**: Soft 3D claymation, 3D render style, deep midnight blue backgrounds with warm orange/yellow highlights.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Surface (Primary Background)**: `#0b1229` (Deep Midnight Blue) - The foundational color for the night theme.
- **Surface-Bright**: `#323851` - Used for containers and slightly elevated surfaces.
- **Primary (Action)**: `#ff9f1c` (Citrus Orange) - Used for primary buttons, active icons, and branding.
- **Secondary (Highlights)**: `#ffd23f` (Soft Yellow) - Used for star glows, secondary actions, and high-contrast text.
- **On-Surface**: `#ffffff` (Pure White) - High contrast text for titles.
- **On-Surface-Variant**: `#94a3b8` (Slate Grey) - Secondary text, captions, and inactive states.

## 3. Typography
- **Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Primary), HarmonyOS Sans (Chinese Fallback)
- **Scale**:
  - **Headline-LG**: 32px / Bold (Title screens, welcome greetings)
  - **Headline-MD**: 24px / Semi-Bold (Page titles)
  - **Title-LG**: 20px / Medium (Card titles, section headers)
  - **Body-MD**: 16px / Regular (Story text, descriptions)
  - **Label-SM**: 12px / Medium (Tags, metadata)

## 4. Components & Layout
### Top Bar
- **Style**: Transparent or semi-transparent with blur.
- **Content**: Left-aligned branding or avatar, right-aligned notifications/search.

### Bottom Navigation
- **Icons**: `auto_stories` (Library), `stars` (Vault), `settings` (Settings).
- **Style**: Rounded corners, deep blue background, orange active indicators.

### Story Cards
- **Structure**: Stacked or floating. Rounded corners (8px-12px).
- **Metadata**: Star rating, duration (icon + text), tags (small pill shapes).
- **Actions**: "Start Reading" prominent button.

### Reading Page (Immersive Mode)
- **Hierarchy**: Minimalist. Heading -> Tags -> Body Text.
- **Floating Action Button (FAB)**: Simple circle with play icon for audio narration.
- **Narration Highlight**: Subtle orange text glow or soft underline for current sentence.

## 5. Visual Language
- **Corner Radius**: `ROUND_EIGHT` (8px-12px) for a soft, friendly feel.
- **Shadows**: Large, soft radial gradients to blend 3D assets into background.
- **Decorations**: Twinkling stars, crescent moons, and fireflies.
- **IP Integration**: Illustrations should have soft internal glows and no hard edges.

## 6. Content Standards
- **Language**: Simplified Chinese.
- **Tone**: Warm, encouraging, and gentle.
- **Safety**: No horror, violence, or blood. Focus on classics and world folklore.
